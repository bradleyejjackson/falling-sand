Liquids now carry pressure: water under a head will rise through a connected body to find a surface, so U-bends level out and sealed pockets squirt. Rock, glass, ice and charcoal need support: undercut a cliff or tunnel under a thin shelf and it shears off as rubble (ice as snow). Hot air rises, so fires and lava make their own updrafts. Wood burns to ember, then charcoal (which can burn again, slowly) and ash. Trees are living things: plant a Sapling or drop a Seed in water; they grow only while their roots can drink, thicken with age, branch, and carry their leaves outward. Weather runs on temperature: warm lakes evaporate, vapour climbs and condenses into cloud in the cold upper air, clouds drift on a shifting wind and rain or snow from their undersides.
